# Phase 0B — Vague need (no name yet)


If they named a person, skip this and go 0A → 0.5.
If they only have a problem, **infer the distill target**. At most two
follow-ups. If the need is already clear, recommend without asking.

### Step 1: locate the demand

| Demand | They might say | Framework direction |
|---|---|---|
| Decision & judgment | worse choices, analysis paralysis, always pick wrong | multiple models, inversion, probability |
| Expression & writing | can't make it clear, nobody reads it, boring prose | Feynman-simple, story, analogy |
| Startup & business | indie product, business model stuck, no PMF | first principles, leverage, product restraint |
| Teaching & transmission | nobody follows the lesson, students don't get it | known→unknown, metaphor, minimum necessary |
| Critical thinking | keep getting sold, want to see through claims | falsification, evolutionary view, bias check |
| Content creation | no reach, don't know what to make, no voice | attention, test-and-iterate, audience |
| Life strategy | career fog, no time, anxiety | long game, leverage of choice, compounding |
| Risk & uncertainty | black swans, investing losses, too timid / too reckless | antifragility, convex bets, tail risk |
| Design & product | weak UX, no distinctiveness, can't cut | minimalism, user model, constraint as craft |
| Humor & presence | flat voice, want it funnier, too solemn | mismatch, expectation break, self-owning authority |

Follow-ups exist only to split lookalikes (business decision vs life
decision). One round to locate the row, then recommend.

### Step 2: person skill or topic skill?

- Need a *someone's* way of seeing → **person engram**
- Need a field's toolkit (value investing, product restraint) → **topic
  skill** (`[topic]-framework/`, same directory shape). Follow the
  phase-variant table in `references/special-scenarios.md`. Consensus +
  school splits instead of one mind; neutral exposition.
- Unsure → include both kinds in the 2–3 options.

### Step 3: recommend 2–3 candidates

**Source A — already installed.** Scan `~/.tink/skills/engrams/` and
`~/.tink/skills/personas-skillset/`. Read each SKILL.md description.
A match that already exists is cheaper than a new distill. Empty scan
→ skip to B.

**Source B — new distill.** Match the "Framework direction" column to a
person or topic. Name the *model*, not the celebrity.

Card format (every candidate):

```
### Candidate N: [name or topic]  — already installed / needs distill

**Lens:** [one line — how they see]
**Why it fits:** [map to their stated demand]
**Limit:** [what this lens will not help]
```

Rules: max 3; existing skills first; candidates must differ; every card
has a limit; pick by lens fit, not fame.

### Step 4: they pick

- Existing skill → activate it. Distill is done.
- New person → Phase 0A details → 0.5.
- None of these → one more 0B loop, or they name someone.

**Calibration (paper):** "I keep picking wrong products" → Demand =
Startup & business (product / PMF), not Life strategy. One optional
split: shipping a product vs picking a career. Then ≤3 candidates whose
lenses actually hit product judgment (e.g. a Jobs-style taste/cut lens,
a Graham/Munger inversion lens, a first-principles build lens) — each
with a limit.
